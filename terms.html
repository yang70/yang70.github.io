Personal deployment

Sandy OpenClaw is an independently operated personal assistant maintained by GitHub user yang70. This website describes its Google integration; it does not offer public registration or access to the assistant.

Authorized use

Only an authorized owner may connect a Google account or use the private assistant deployment. Connect only accounts and files you are entitled to access. Review Google's consent screen and the privacy policy before authorizing access or requesting a routine.

The integration reads Gmail, Drive, Docs and Sheets data. Optional spreadsheet editing requires additional Google consent and is confined by the local helper to files explicitly approved by the owner. Sending email, editing Google Docs and deleting Drive files remain outside its permission set. The owner controls queries, writes, schedules and message destinations.

Assistant output

AI-generated answers and summaries can be incomplete or incorrect. Check relevant source messages, documents and spreadsheet cells before relying on an answer. Service availability depends on the owner's computer, network, Google APIs, AI providers and chosen message service; uninterrupted operation is not promised.

Service and data controls

The owner may disable the integration, revoke Google authorization or change the personal deployment. Any expanded access requires a separate authorization review. Google, GitHub, AI-provider and message-service terms apply to their respective services.

Contact

General questions can be raised through the GitHub issue tracker. Never post credentials or private Google content in a public issue. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, OpenAI or the OpenClaw maintainers.
